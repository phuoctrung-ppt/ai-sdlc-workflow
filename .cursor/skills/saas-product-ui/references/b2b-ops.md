# Domain pack: B2B ops / admin

**Use when:** internal tools, multi-tenant admin, support consoles, fleet/ops dashboards.

## Aesthetic
- Maximum clarity, minimum brand theater.
- May lean Fluent/Carbon/Atlassian if AGENTS.md locks that DS — honor official packages.
- Status-heavy UIs: consistent semantic colors and sortable tables.

## Dials
- VARIANCE: 2–4
- MOTION: 1–3
- DENSITY: 8–9

## Patterns
- Tenant/workspace switcher always visible when multi-tenant.
- Bulk actions + filters as primary tools.
- Audit logs and permission matrices as tables, not cards.
- Danger zones grouped at bottom of settings with confirm.

## Avoid
- Consumer-playful illustration in admin
- Hiding destructive capability behind unlabeled icons
