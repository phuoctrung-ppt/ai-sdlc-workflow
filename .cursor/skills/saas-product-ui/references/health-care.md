# Domain pack: Health / wellness

**Use when:** clinical tools, patient portals, wellness coaching, health admin (non-marketing).

## Aesthetic
- **Calm, high legibility.** Soft neutrals; limited accent; no aggressive motion.
- Accessibility-first: AA/AAA targets, large hit targets, plain language.
- Avoid gamification of clinical actions.

## Dials
- VARIANCE: 2–4
- MOTION: 1–3
- DENSITY: 5–7 (forms may be airier than eng tools)

## Patterns
- Step clarity for multi-step clinical or intake forms.
- Strong empty/error states; never lose user data silently.
- Role-aware nav (patient vs clinician vs admin).
- Sensitive data: mask by default where appropriate; explicit reveal.

## Avoid
- High-motion scroll experiences
- Alarming red as brand accent (reserve red for true emergencies)
- Cute illustration that reduces trust in clinical contexts
